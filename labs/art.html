2D pictures on a canvas: a subject, painted in a technique, on a surface lit from the side, then converted and given a format. Zoom in (wheel, up to 32×) to see the brush strokes and the support. Every picture is a JSON spec with a seed — the same spec gives the same picture, so a game or an AI can order one (ArtApi in the console).

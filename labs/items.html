How it works

The barrel is the plain JunkKit model; DeformKit (js/behaviors/land/deform/) attaches to it. Every vertex of its sparse shell (from the item's data) is a particle. Edges keep their length (the sheet hardly stretches), pairs across an edge keep the bend (the sheet's stiffness); past the yield limit the new shape stays — the crush is permanent. The press, the supports and the ground push the particles with friction; the barrels in the container push each other. Each barrel runs in its own thread. A click grabs a barrel (up lifts it), the next lets go; W A S D move the camera.

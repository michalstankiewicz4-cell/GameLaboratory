Effects

Effects from Hive Hunger: a shot with a round bow shock, a plasma orb with a fiery tail and a burst, debris, lightning, chain lightning, a liquid bubble that bends the picture like a lens, a black hole that bends the picture around it and pulls the debris in; the swarm's laser, the piercer's beam with its spiral lightning, the storm's forked lightning, the tug's tractor beam, the cutter's flat sheet, the booster's force field, the hyperspace jump, the marker and the subspace tunnel; and the ships' scan wave. In space, with no gravity. Every number of an effect is on a slider; save it under a name and the scene editor plays it on an event. The camera: drag to turn, the wheel zooms.

For AI: EffectsApi in the console (API tab).

Scale

Metres for one unit of the effect (in Hive Hunger a block is 0.5 units). Numbers per frame of 1/60 s, as in the game.

Saved
Name

A saved effect is a named asset: in the scene editor the API command "effect" plays it (an "effect" event).

City

A building creator: houses, tenements, blocks, skyscrapers, offices and supermarkets on any outline, with windows, entrances, shop windows, balconies, cornices and roofs placed from a short description. Near — a real mesh; far — flat walls with the facade baked into textures and parallax, so the windows still look recessed for a fraction of the cost. Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom.

For AI: CityApi in the console (API tab).

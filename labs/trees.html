Tech tree

A game's upgrade or research tree as data: branches with their colours, nodes that grow from their parents (any one of them or all), with a cost, an effect (game stats that add up) and a text in English and Polish. Drawn as a radial pixel-art tree growing up from its core. Edit it with the mouse, try it out with points, check it for nodes nobody can reach.

Shapes: diamond — a trait of the whole (big with an icon), hexagon — a unit or building, circle — a unit's property. Dashed link: either parent is enough; solid across branches: it needs all of them.

For AI: TreeApi in the console (API tab) — load a tree, check it, buy nodes, save it under a name.

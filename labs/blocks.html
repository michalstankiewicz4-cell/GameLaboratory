Blocks

Bodies built of blocks, as in Hive Hunger: rock, iron, ice, sandstone, water, lava and gas in layers. Click the body to bite a hole: water and lava come loose as droplets that hold together into floating blobs, gas as a drifting cloud — in space there is no gravity. Solid blocks take their look from the materials lab. Right-drag or Shift-drag turns the camera, the wheel zooms.

For AI: BlocksApi in the console (API tab).

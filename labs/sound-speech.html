Speech

Voices for game characters, made live — no recordings. The browser's speech synthesis reads the text aloud in a real language; the synthetic voice speaks it as a character's babble, from formants (the resonances of the throat), like the voices of many games; a whisper murmurs without words. The synthetic voices go through effects — radio, robot, echo, distance — and the engine's mixer (the speech bus).

For AI: VoiceApi in the console (API tab).

Ctrl+Enter says the text. Commas and full stops make pauses; a question goes up at the end.

Effects and ambience

The game's sound effects and the sound of places, made live from code — no audio files: explosions, shots, thunder, water, impacts, birds, wind, rain, the sea, fire, an engine. An ambience is layers: steady sounds and sounds now and then (birds in a forest, thunder in a storm). Everything plays through the engine's mixer — effects on the effects bus, ambiences on the ambience bus — and can stand in space around the listener.

For AI: SfxApi in the console (API tab).

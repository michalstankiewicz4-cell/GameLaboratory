How it computes

Each element's angle follows from the motor's angle through the joints: a mesh (the teeth ratio, the direction reverses), a chain and a belt (the same direction), a cardan joint (from the cross's geometry — the ratio changes during a turn), a planetary gear (Willis). The motor accelerates the whole machine's reduced inertia; the brake loads the output, each element's torque follows from the power and the joints' efficiency.

The items' and the machine's data (names, properties, materials) are in Polish, like the models' data.

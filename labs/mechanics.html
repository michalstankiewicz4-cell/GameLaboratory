Mechanics

One machine of elements joined by ports.

How it computes

Each element's angle is computed from the motor angle along the connections: gear mesh (tooth ratio, direction change), chain and belt (same direction), Cardan joint (from the cross geometry — the ratio changes during a revolution), planetary gear (Willis formula), rack, lead screw and crank (rotation ↔ translation). The motor accelerates the reduced inertia of the whole machine; the brake loads the output, the torque in every element follows from power and connection efficiencies. In Physics mode every part has its own motion and the connections are constraints with backlash, elasticity and friction.

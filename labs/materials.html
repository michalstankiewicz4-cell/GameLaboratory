Materials

One library of materials for every lab and game: a material is a short description — the kind of surface, colours, the size of the pattern, roughness, metalness, bumps and ageing — and the maps (colour, normal, roughness, metalness, ambient occlusion) are generated from it, seamless, the same every time. Drag to turn the camera, wheel to zoom; move the sun in the Light window to see the bumps.

For AI: MaterialApi in the console (API tab).
